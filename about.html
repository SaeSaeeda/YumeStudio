About Saeeda

I build what you feel,
not just what you say.

I'm a developer and designer who has always lived at the intersection of art and technology. Web design is where those two worlds finally made sense together.

Before I write a single line of code, I build a scene. I sit with your vision — your event, your feeling, your people — and I imagine it. I'm an overthinker, and in this work, that's a gift. By the time I start building, I already know what it should feel like.

The moment I love most is when you see it for the first time and say that's exactly it — or better yet, something you couldn't have imagined on your own.

When I'm not building websites, I'm travelling somewhere new, painting, writing poetry, or standing still in a forest listening to the ocean. I practise archery and karate. I read everything. I love flowers. I believe the way you pay attention to the world outside your work shows up in everything you make.

Yume Studio exists for people who have a clear vision and need exactly the right person to bring it to life. If that's you — I'd love to hear about it.
